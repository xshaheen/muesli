## Imla 0.8.5

Imla 0.8.5 is the first release under the Imla name. It adds offline Arabic transcription, a Modes system for dictation cleanup, chat and cleaned transcripts for meetings, a redesigned meeting panel, and Claude as a summary provider. Meetings now keep your microphone and the other speakers on separate tracks, so a re-transcribed meeting no longer mixes you with them.

## Upgrading from Muesli

Imla is a new app identity, so macOS and Imla treat it as a fresh install:

- Your history stays in `~/Library/Application Support/Muesli/`, and nothing moves automatically. To carry it across, quit both apps and run, once, before first launch:

  ```bash
  mv ~/Library/Application\ Support/Muesli ~/Library/Application\ Support/Imla
  cd ~/Library/Application\ Support/Imla && for f in muesli.db*; do mv "$f" "imla.db${f#muesli.db}"; done
  mv ~/.cache/muesli ~/.cache/imla   # keeps downloaded speech models
  ```

- Grant Microphone, Accessibility, Input Monitoring, and audio capture again; macOS tracks permissions per app.
- Sign in to ChatGPT and Google again. Their tokens stay in the Keychain under the old name.
- This build does not check for updates.

## Dictation

- **Modes** replace Writing Styles. A mode is chosen from the app or website you are dictating into, and sets the cleanup instructions and whether to press Return after pasting. Existing Writing Styles migrate once, automatically.
- **Custom instructions** apply to every cleanup prompt, and to meeting notes, titles, and transcript cleanup.
- **Language routing:** dictation follows your keyboard's intended language, and one spoken-language profile is shared by dictation and meetings. Mixed-language repair turns on by itself for bilingual profiles.
- **Paste shortcut** follows the active keyboard layout, or a shortcut you set.
- **Hosted dictation** through OpenAI or OpenRouter now pastes the provider's transcript and records that model in history. It used to be transcribed locally as well and the provider's result discarded.
- A redesigned caret-following indicator shows preparing, recording, and done states, and a clear failure when speech was heard but no text came out.
- Your custom dictionary is shown to Whisper and to the cleanup model.

## Meetings

- **Separate tracks:** recordings keep your microphone and system audio apart. Re-transcription labels you as You and diarizes only the other speakers, and runs as a cancellable background job with progress.
- **Mute** your microphone from the meeting panel; while muted, the recording and transcript get silence instead of your room.
- **Meeting panel:** one floating panel with Transcript, Chat, and Notes tabs. Starting from the menu bar or a calendar event opens only the panel, not the main window.
- **Hidden from screen sharing:** the meeting panel and dictation overlays are excluded from screen capture.
- **Chat** with a meeting from its detail view or the floating panel, during or after the call.
- **Cleaned transcripts:** for bilingual meetings, the finished transcript is repaired by your summary provider and notes are regenerated from it. The raw transcript is kept.
- Recordings keep the echo-cancelled microphone, and a resumed meeting keeps its whole recording. Recordings can expire per kind.
- If the chosen microphone goes silent, Imla fails over to a working one. Interrupted system audio recovers on its own.
- Copying notes or a transcript adds the meeting's title, date, duration, word count, and template, the same header exports use.
- Arabic meetings keep Arabic output, and right-to-left text renders in its natural direction.

## Transcription models

- **Cohere Arabic** for offline Arabic dictation and meeting transcription.
- A separate final transcript model can be chosen for meetings.
- The Qwen3 speech backend is retired; anyone who had it selected is moved to a supported model.
- Idle speech and cleanup models are released from memory instead of being held for the life of the app.
- Voice notes import in any audio container macOS can decode.

## Summaries and cleanup

- **Anthropic API** and **Claude Code** are new summary providers; the Anthropic API also serves cleanup. Claude presets are Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1, and Haiku 5.5.
- **Custom LLM** keys can come from a command (for example a password manager), and requests can carry custom headers.
- OpenAI summaries and meeting chat opt out of server-side storage.

## Interface

- Settings, Models, Shortcuts, and About move into their own window, which opens on the current Space.
- A refreshed design: Inter text, a coral accent, continuous corners, and Reduce Motion that stops motion.
- The sidebar scrolls, so a long folder tree fits the window.
- Insights counts dictated words apart from meeting words.
- Calendar access can be turned on from Settings.
- Local session diagnostics record metadata only, and can be cleared or exported.

## Fixes

- Deleting a meeting no longer fails with `NOT NULL constraint failed: meetings.visual_context` on older databases.
- Quitting or cancelling during a re-transcription no longer waits for the whole recording to be processed.
- Caret lookup no longer deadlocks Imla after a focus change.
- Floating panels return to every Space when the window server drops them, including over full-screen apps.

Dictation and meeting transcription are local by default. Hosted dictation sends audio to the provider you select. Hosted cleanup, chat, and summaries send the text those features need, only when you choose such a provider. Claude Code runs through the Claude CLI on your machine, which sends the transcript to Anthropic.
