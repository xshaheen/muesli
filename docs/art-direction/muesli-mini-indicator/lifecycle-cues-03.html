Muesli / Indicator direction 03

Hear the state.

Three compact sounds make the lifecycle legible without looking: recording began, capture ended, and finished text arrived.

Dictation lifecycle
00 · armed

Preparing

Silent until the microphone is truly active.

01 · start cue

Listening

Short rising click confirms capture.

02 · stop cue

Capture ended

Short falling tap marks the handoff.

03 · generating

Transcribing

Orb animates; no repeating sound.

04 · success cue

Text delivered

Distinct resolved note confirms success.

Success gate

Play only for real delivery.

  • ✓Non-empty transcript pasted into the intended app.
  • ✓Voice-note output successfully saved.
  • ×Empty, cancelled, failed, stale, or target-changed output.
Current-source finding

Two cues already exist.

Muesli already plays `Tink` at stream activation and `Purr` after capture/audio restoration. The implementation delta is to rename the stop semantic clearly, add a deduplicated success event after delivery, and prewarm its distinct sound.
