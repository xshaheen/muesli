One object, three sizes:
pill, row, panel.

The recording capsule and the floating transcript panel merge into one glass object. Pill at rest: a breathing coral dot and the live clock. Row on hover: the wave, pause/resume, stop, and one button that opens the panel. Panel on that button: the row becomes the header, tabs for Transcript · Chat · My notes sit under it, the body is the live feed, chat, or notes — resizable, draggable, and it folds back to the pill from ⤡. One position, one remembered choice, no second window.

Decisions

Mergethe recording capsule and the floating transcript panel become one object with three sizes; there is no second window, no second saved position
Pill → Rowhover opens the row (0.16 s); the dot + clock stay put and the controls unfold on the side with room (mirrored order when the pill sits against the right edge), so nothing lands under the resting pointer; the pointer leaving folds it back after 0.4 s; a press or drag holds it; after a minimize the row waits until the pointer has left the pill once; Reduce Motion → instant; the row never opens the panel by itself
Row → Panelthe ⤢ button opens the panel; the panel is sticky until ⤡ minimizes it; remembered in meeting_panel_open (nil → the entry point decides as today: pill/hotkey/status bar open it, calendar and auto-detect don't; after the first user toggle the remembered choice wins; starts that open the meeting document — Quick Note, Join & Record, calendar items — always rest as the pill; only a user's open/minimize writes the choice, never the end-of-call fold)
Anchorthe 72 pt base pill frame is the anchor and meeting_recording_panel_center stores its center; the held corner is chosen once per recording (trailing edge in the right half of the display, bottom edge in the lower half) and kept across drags, minimize and reopen, so a panel dragged across the screen reopens where it was left; row and panel grow from that corner, clamped 12 pt inside; dragging any size moves the anchor by the pointer delta; resizing the panel keeps the held corner; minimizing returns to the anchor
Panel header30 pt: dot + clock, the spark wave stretched across the free width (same engine, more bars), then one trailing cluster pause · stop ‖ minimize behind a hairline — the clock and the controls keep their row positions relative to the edges; the tab strip and body are the old transcript panel restyled in the Spark glass with coral for You and 92 % ink for Others
Finalizingthe object folds to the pill and shows the status word (amber 10 pt), widening once to fit the widest word while holding the held edge; hover still opens the row with pause/stop/open-panel at 36 %; the panel is not available; closes when the meeting is saved
Record pillsame footprint and chrome: “● Record” → “● Starting…” (dimmed, inert) → “● 00:00”; the Record pill stays hidden while the object is on screen, including finalizing
Menus / settingsstatus bar “Show/Hide Live Transcript” becomes “Open/Minimize Meeting Panel”; “Show transcript on hover” and meeting_panel_origin are retired (legacy keys ignored)
Accessibilitypill = one button “Meeting recording, 12:34, recording/paused” with custom actions Open panel · Pause/Resume · Stop; row/panel = group with labelled buttons; the panel keeps its no-key-window rule (orderFront, never makeKey) so typing stays in the call; state changes announced once
Not adoptedclick-on-body toggles; a separate mini transcript window; auto-opening the panel on hover; keeping the panel's independent saved origin
