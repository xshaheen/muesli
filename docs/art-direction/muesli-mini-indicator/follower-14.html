The dot that follows.

The Mini adopts the follower model: a persistent idle seed near the focused text context that glides with the caret, steps aside while you type or scroll, hides on Escape, and can be dragged onto a window to ride with it. Hovering shows the hotkey; a selection shows what holding it will do; hands‑free mode toasts. Recording is lit by seeded sparks instead of a scrolling history.
