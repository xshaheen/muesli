One capsule, two sizes:
the meeting panel folds into its timer.

The Meeting Recording Panel becomes the same 22 pt Contextual Spark glass object as the Record pill and the Mini's capsule. Minimized it is the Record pill with a live clock: a pulsing coral dot and mm:ss. Expanded it unfolds in place into one low row — dot + clock, live wave, pause/resume, stop, transcript, collapse. Click the pill to expand, ‹ to collapse; the choice sticks across recordings; drag moves it in either size. The legacy 224 × 46 two‑line HUD bar goes away.

Decisions

Replacesthe 224 × 46 HUD bar (transcript · pause · bars · clock/status · stop) — ❚❚00:00Meeting recording■
Toggleminimized: click anywhere on the pill expands; expanded: only the ‹ chevron collapses — the body is drag‑only so a missed pause tap never folds it; 6 pt drag threshold unchanged; ⌥‑click / right‑click still asks to discard
Rememberednew config key meeting_recording_panel_expanded (default true → the first recording opens expanded); saved on every toggle; a new recording opens in the last state
Anchor on resizethe minimized pill frame is the canonical anchor and its center is what meeting_recording_panel_center stores; the capsule extends trailing from that frame and clamps 12 pt inside the pill's display (so a right‑edge pill unfolds leftwards); if the visible transcript panel sits on the trailing side it extends leading instead; dragging the capsule moves the anchor by the pointer delta; collapse returns to the anchor
Clockmm:ss in 72 pt; h:mm:ss steps to 86 pt once and stays; tabular digits; paused freezes it at 70 % ink
Pausedamber dot without halo; wave slot reads “Paused”; play glyph; pill shows the amber dot + frozen clock; status‑bar and hotkey pause mirror here
Finalizingamber 0.9 s pulse; Finalizing / Transcribing / Cleaning / Titling / Summarizing in the wave slot (expanded) or in place of the clock (pill, widens to fit); pause/stop/transcript at 36 %; collapse stays live; closes on completion
Transcriptsame toggle button (coral when shown); “show transcript on hover” applies to the expanded capsule only — hovering the minimized pill never pops the transcript; collapsing leaves a shown transcript where it is
Record pillidentical footprint and chrome, so the hand‑off is a label swap: “● Record” → “● Starting…” (dimmed, inert, while capture starts) → “● 00:00”; shared saved center unchanged; the Record pill stays hidden while the recording pill is on screen, including finalizing
Motion0.16 s ease‑out width morph with controls fading in after; dot halo breathes 1.6 s; Reduce Motion → instant size change, static dot; Reduce Transparency → opaque #211f1e; Increase Contrast → 2 pt 82 % edge
Accessibilitypill = one element, role button, label “Meeting recording, 12:34, recording/paused”, custom actions Expand · Pause/Resume · Stop; expanded = group with labelled buttons, key chain pause → stop → transcript → collapse; state changes announced once
Not adoptedhover‑to‑expand (fights drag and the transcript hover); click‑on‑body collapse (mis‑click risk during calls); a taller two‑line panel; a second saved position; controls on the Dictation Mini
