A Record pill,
only when there is a meeting.

The floating button returns for meetings only. It appears while a recognised meeting app is actively in use, sits at the saved Meeting Recording Panel position, and starts a recording with one click — then hands off in place to the panel that owns pause, stop and transcript. No idle chrome, no dictation semantics.
