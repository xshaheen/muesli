Motion policy
  • 01
    Resolve the focused insertion caret through macOS Accessibility geometry.
  • 02
    Poll while preparing or recording; ignore movement below four points.

    Freeze during processing, then reacquire the post-insertion caret once for terminal feedback.
  • 04
    Flip quadrants before clamping so the capsule does not cover upcoming text.

Parent · fixed floating pill

Always locatable and directly draggable, but it consumes persistent space and asks the eye to leave the active work.

Child · contextual spark

Starts with no idle footprint, materializes near intent, expands only as much as the state requires, and dismisses itself.
