Muesli / Indicator direction 04

Failure has a sound.

A restrained low cue says the dictation did not complete. Cancellation stays silent because the user already knows what they chose.

Dictation failed

The visual remains compact but persists long enough to understand; the sound plays once when failure becomes terminal.

Failure contract

One cue. One terminal owner.

01
Recording failure

The microphone stream cannot start or terminates unexpectedly.

02
Transcription failure

ASR, cleanup, or generation reaches a terminal error.

03
Delivery failure

Finished text cannot reach the intended target; saving alone is not presented as delivered success.

04
Deduplicate

The session's winning terminal outcome emits the cue, not every internal catch block.

Failure stays silent

Expected user intent

Cancel, explicit discard, and test mode do not produce the failure cue. Empty/no-speech remains a separate neutral state to decide later rather than being mislabeled as a system failure.
